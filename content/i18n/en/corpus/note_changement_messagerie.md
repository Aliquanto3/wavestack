# Exemplia email change note

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this note describes no real company. -->

This note explains the move from version 1 to version 2 of the email charter, effective since 1 January 2026.

Exemplia changed its email provider on 1 January 2026, after a tender run in 2025. The new provider, hosted within the European Union, offers a larger attachment limit and a faster search in archives. This change led to the revision of the email charter, from its version 1 to its version 2.

Three main differences are worth remembering. First, the maximum size of a message rises from 10 Mo to 25 Mo. Second, the retention period in the main mailbox rises from 1 year to 3 years, to meet a new regulatory obligation of the sector. Finally, occasional and reasonable personal use of the professional mailbox, previously not tolerated, is now accepted.

The migration of mailboxes took place over three weekends in December 2025, with no message loss reported. Any question about the migration goes to support, on extension 3100; any question about the new rules of the charter goes to the security unit.

Version 1 of the charter remains available as an archive, to understand decisions made before this change, but it no longer applies since 1 January 2026.
