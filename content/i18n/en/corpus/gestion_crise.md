# Exemplia crisis management procedure

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

This procedure takes over from the incident reporting procedure as soon as a level 3 incident is declared, or when several Exemplia sites are affected at the same time.

The crisis unit convenes in under 2 hours, day and night, chaired by the chief executive officer or, in their absence, by the duty crisis director. To know who to call at night, the on-call contacts annex lists the numbers to reach, depending on the time and the area concerned.

The crisis unit includes, at minimum, the chief information security officer, the communications director, the data protection officer when personal data is involved, and a representative of the legal department. It meets in the head office's crisis room, or by videoconference if access to the head office is compromised.

A crisis log is opened at the first meeting: it records every decision, its author and its time, and serves as the basis for the lessons-learned report required by the incident procedure. The crisis unit shares a situation update every 2 hours with the directions concerned, and a single message to employees is approved before any release.

The crisis is closed once the unit finds the situation under control and corrective measures under way; the closure is recorded in the crisis log.
