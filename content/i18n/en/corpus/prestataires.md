# Exemplia supplier security policy

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this policy describes no real company. -->

Exemplia places every supplier who handles company information into one of two tiers: critical, when it hosts data or connects to the internal network, or standard for the others. The tier is set by the supplier unit before the contract is signed, and reviewed every year.

A critical supplier is audited before signature, then every 3 years, and must hold an ISO 27001 certification or equivalent. A standard supplier answers an annual security questionnaire. In both cases, the signed contract carries the security clauses of Exemplia's model, detailed in a separate document.

Data entrusted to a supplier stays hosted within the European Union, unless a written exception is approved by the security committee. A supplier does not subcontract on its own without Exemplia's agreement, and every subcontractor it uses is recorded in the subcontractor register kept by the supplier unit.

Any security incident at a supplier must be reported to Exemplia, within the delay set in its contract. The supplier unit, reachable on extension 3200, tracks the list of active suppliers and their tier, and ends the relationship if an audit fails twice in a row.

At the end of a contract, the supplier erases Exemplia's data and confirms it in writing within the delay set by the contract's clauses.
