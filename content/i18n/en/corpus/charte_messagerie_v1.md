# Exemplia email charter, version 1 (2024)

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this charter describes no real company. -->

This version of the email charter, in force since January 2024, is replaced by version 2: it is kept here as an archive, to understand past decisions.

Exemplia's professional mailbox is reserved for professional use; occasional personal use is not tolerated and may lead to a reminder from the manager. Each message, with its attachments, does not exceed 10 Mo; beyond that, a temporary sharing link must be used.

Sent and received messages are kept for 1 year in the mailbox, then automatically archived and removed from the main mailbox. A message deleted by mistake can be restored by support within 30 days of its deletion, beyond which it is lost.

A message sent by mistake to the wrong external recipient is reported to the incident response team within the hour, especially if it contains confidential information or personal data.

Automatically forwarding a professional mailbox to a personal address is forbidden, as is using a personal mailbox for a professional exchange.
