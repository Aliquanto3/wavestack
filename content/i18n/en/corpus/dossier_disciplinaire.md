# Exemplia disciplinary procedure

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

This document describes the steps of a disciplinary procedure at Exemplia; it is classed confidential and its access is reserved to human resources, management and, for a file concerning them, the employee themselves.

A behavioural issue or a breach leads, depending on its severity, to one of these measures: an oral reminder, a written warning placed on file, a 1- to 5-day unpaid suspension, or dismissal for misconduct. Every measure other than an oral reminder is preceded by a meeting where the employee may be assisted by a person of their choice.

The meeting is called in writing at least 5 working days before its date, with the reason for the meeting. The decision is notified in writing within 10 working days after the meeting, with the internal appeal routes.

A written warning stays on file for 3 years; past that delay, it is no longer taken into account for a later measure, unless the same breach recurs before it is erased.

An employee's full disciplinary file is kept for 5 years after they leave Exemplia, then anonymised, in line with the personal data retention policy.
