# Security clauses for Exemplia supplier contracts

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this model of clauses describes no real company. -->

This model completes the supplier security policy: it lists the clauses the supplier unit inserts into every supply contract, according to the supplier's tier.

Notification clause: a supplier reports to Exemplia any security incident touching Exemplia's data or systems within 24 hours of its discovery, even in case of doubt.

Audit clause: Exemplia, or an auditor it appoints, may check on site or remotely that the security clauses are respected, with 10 working days' notice for a standard supplier and without notice for a critical supplier in case of a suspected incident.

Encryption clause: any Exemplia data stored by the supplier is encrypted at rest with an algorithm of at least 256 bits, and in transit with an up-to-date protocol.

Return and erasure clause: at the end of the contract, or on Exemplia's request, the supplier returns the data in a usable format then erases it from all its systems, including its backups, within 30 days, and provides written proof of it.

Subcontracting clause: the supplier informs Exemplia before using a subcontractor, who is then added to the subcontractor register and carries the same clauses.

A clause not respected, flagged twice by an audit, lets Exemplia terminate the contract without compensation for the supplier.
