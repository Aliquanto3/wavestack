# Exemplia physical access policy

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this policy describes no real company. -->

Each Exemplia site is divided into 3 zones: normal (reception, open space), restricted (sensitive meeting rooms, technical rooms) and secure (server room, crisis room). A personal badge, issued to every employee from their first day, grants access to the zones their role needs.

Access to a restricted zone requires the zone manager's approval, recorded in the badge system; access to a secure zone also requires the chief information security officer's approval, and passing through a double-door airlock.

Every visitor receives a temporary badge at reception, after an ID check, and must be escorted by an employee in any restricted or secure zone. The visitor badge is returned at reception on leaving; the visitor register is kept for 90 days.

A lost badge is reported immediately to reception, which deactivates it within the hour; a new badge is issued after an identity check. An employee's badge is deactivated within 24 hours of them leaving Exemplia.

Every access, badge accepted or refused, is logged and kept for 6 months, consulted during a security investigation or an access rights review.
