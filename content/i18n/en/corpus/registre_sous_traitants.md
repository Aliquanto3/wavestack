# Exemplia subcontractor register

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this register describes no real company. -->

This register, kept by the supplier unit and cited by the model of security clauses, lists every subcontractor used by an Exemplia supplier: its name, its function, the country where it hosts or processes data, and the tier of the supplier that uses it.

NuageSud, hosting of the accounting servers, France, critical supplier. ArchivePlus, paper archiving of personnel files, Belgium, standard supplier. TransliX, translation of commercial documents, France, standard supplier. SupportNuit, level 1 phone support outside working hours, Portugal, critical supplier. ImpressionPro, printing of communication documents, France, standard supplier.

Each entry of the register is reviewed every quarter by the supplier unit: a subcontractor no longer in use is removed, and a new subcontractor is added only after its own security guarantees have been checked, following the clauses of the model.

The register is given to the supervisory authority on request, as part of a check on personal data protection, and to the security committee every quarter, with the list of audited suppliers.

A subcontractor hosted outside the European Union only appears in the register with the written exception set by the supplier policy, attached to its entry.
