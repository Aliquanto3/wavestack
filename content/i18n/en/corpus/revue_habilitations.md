# Exemplia access rights review

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

An access right is a permission to use an application, a shared folder or a physical zone, described in the physical access policy for premises. This document sets the rules for their regular review.

Every manager reviews, each quarter, the list of access rights of their team: they confirm those still needed and flag those to withdraw, for example after a role change. The review is recorded in the access management tool, with the date and the manager's name.

An employee's account is deactivated the day they leave Exemplia, and their access rights are withdrawn within 48 hours; an account with no identified owner, called an orphan account, is suspended as soon as detected then deleted after a check.

Once a year, the security team runs a full review of every sensitive access right, those that grant access to personal data, accounting, or secure zones, independently of managers' quarterly reviews. Gaps found are corrected within 15 working days.

An external audit may cover the access rights review; the register of reviews for the past two years is then presented as proof of effective access control.
