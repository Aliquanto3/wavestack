# Exemplia on-call contacts annex

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this annex describes no real company. -->

This annex of the crisis management procedure lists the contacts to reach outside working hours, day and night, depending on the area concerned.

Security on-call, to call first for any major incident at night or at the weekend: the single on-call number is 06 07 08 09 10, followed by an answering service that forwards to the person on duty that week. The rotation changes every Monday at 9 a.m.; the person on call for the week is announced on the intranet.

Infrastructure on-call, for a server or network failure: extension 4100, then 06 11 12 13 14 if there is no answer within 15 minutes. Communications on-call, to prepare an urgent external message: extension 4200. Legal on-call, for a regulatory notification question: extension 4300.

If the security on-call person does not answer within 30 minutes, the call is escalated directly to the chief information security officer, then to the chief executive officer if they too stay unreachable.

Every on-call call is logged, with the time, the reason and the person reached, in the register kept by the operations team, consulted during the lessons-learned report that follows any level 2 or 3 incident.
