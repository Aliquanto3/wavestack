# Exemplia security training

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

Every Exemplia employee takes a 2-hour online information security training, then passes a quiz with at least 8 correct answers out of 10 to obtain it. A new hire takes it within their first month; it is renewed every year for everyone.

The training covers reporting incidents, the password policy, information classification, and good practices for email and generative AI. Failing the quiz, after two attempts, leads to a meeting with the direct manager and a new session within the month.

A phishing simulation campaign is run every quarter, on a sample drawn at random among employees. Whoever clicks the simulated link immediately receives a short explanation and a 20-minute extra training; no sanction applies to a first successful simulated attack.

The quiz pass rate and the click rate on simulations are presented every quarter to the security committee, without naming the employees concerned.

Human resources keeps the list of trainings taken, useful for the access rights review and for the annual review's future training needs.
