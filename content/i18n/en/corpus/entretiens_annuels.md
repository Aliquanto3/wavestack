# Exemplia annual review procedure

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this procedure describes no real company. -->

This document describes Exemplia's annual review, led by each employee's direct manager; it is classed as a human resources document.

The review takes place every year in the first quarter, between January and March. The employee first fills in a self-assessment, within the 2 weeks before the set date, then the review is held with only the direct manager present, for at least 45 minutes.

The review covers how the past year's goals were met, training needs, and the goals for the coming year, set by common agreement. It also serves as the basis for a possible promotion proposal within the compensation grid, then examined by the compensation committee.

The minutes, written by the manager and reviewed by the employee, are signed by both parties within 10 working days after the review. If there is disagreement over part of the minutes, the employee may attach a written note to it.

Minutes are kept for 3 years by human resources, to track how an employee's goals and training needs evolve over several years.
