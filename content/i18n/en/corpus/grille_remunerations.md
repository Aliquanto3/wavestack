# Exemplia compensation grid

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this grid describes no real company. -->

This document describes Exemplia's compensation structure; it is classed confidential and its access is reserved to human resources and management.

The grid has 5 categories, from category A (entry-level roles) to category E (management), each split into 3 steps based on time in the role. Moving from one step to the next is automatic after 2 years, unless frozen in writing for a reason. Moving from one category to another results from a promotion decided after the annual review.

Each category matches a gross annual salary range, revised once a year by the compensation committee, made up of general management and human resources management. The revision takes effect on 1 April.

An annual bonus, calculated on individual performance and Exemplia's results, is added to the base salary for categories C, D and E; it is paid in May, after the accounts are closed.

Any question about one's own position in the grid is addressed to one's human resources manager; the full grid, with amounts, is never shared outside human resources and management, to protect the confidentiality of individual pay.
