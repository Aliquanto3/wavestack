# UK subsidiary visitor badge policy (English-only document)

<!-- Fictitious text written for WaveStack. Exemplia is an imaginary organisation: this policy describes no real company. This document exists in English only, as it is specific to Exemplia's UK subsidiary. -->

This policy applies only to Exemplia's United Kingdom subsidiary, in Manchester; it exists in English only, as the local reception team works in that language.

Every visitor to the Manchester office receives a dated visitor badge at reception, after showing a photo ID and signing the visitor register. The badge must be worn visibly at all times on site, and the visitor must be escorted by an Exemplia employee in any restricted or secure zone, exactly as in the general physical access policy.

A visitor badge is valid only for the day it is issued and the specific floor stated on it. A visitor who needs access to another floor returns to reception for a new badge. Badges are collected back at reception when the visitor leaves; a badge not returned is reported to the site's security officer within one hour.

The visitor register, with the visitor's name, company, host and times of arrival and departure, is kept for 90 days, in line with the data retention rules of the physical access policy, then deleted.

Contractors working on site for more than one day receive a longer-term contractor badge instead, issued under the supplier security policy rather than this one.
